Sizing & Capacity

The Small Pill Pod is sized to hold about a week's supply of small capsules and tablets, split across its three compartments.

The Big Pill Pod is sized to hold about a week's supply of medium and large capsules or softgels.

The Powder Pod is sized to hold about a week's supply of most powders — greens, creatine, and collagen. For protein powder, you may need multiple pods.

The Single Lid is compatible with all three Pilr pod types: Powder Pod, Big Pill Pod, and Small Pill Pod. One lid, any pod.
