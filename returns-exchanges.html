Returns & exchanges

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

We want you to love your PILR system. If it is not right for you, here is how returns and exchanges work.

1. 30-day guarantee

Love it or return it. You can return your order within 30 days of the day it is delivered.

2. What can be returned

Products should be returned in like-new condition [and in their original packaging]. Please wash and fully dry any pods before sending them back. Items that show signs of damage after delivery, or that have been used beyond normal testing, may not qualify for a full refund.

3. How to start a return or exchange

Email us at support@pilrsystems.com with your order number and tell us what you would like to return or exchange. We will reply with the next steps and [a return address or label]. Please do not send a package back before you hear from us.

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we refund the cost of the items to your original payment method within [5–10] business days. Your bank may take a little longer to show the refund. [Shipping costs are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.]

5. Exchanges

Want a different pod, or a different mix? Within 30 days of delivery, email us and we will arrange an exchange. If the new item costs more or less, we will let you know the difference before we send it.

6. Damaged or defective items

If your order arrives damaged, or something is not working as it should, email us within [30] days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

7. Cancelling a pre-order

You can cancel a pre-order anytime before it ships. Email us with your order number and we will cancel it and refund you in full to your original payment method.

8. Contact us

Questions about a return or exchange? Email support@pilrsystems.com or use our contact page.
