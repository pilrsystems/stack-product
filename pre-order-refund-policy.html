Pre-order refund policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

pilr is currently available to pre-order. This policy explains how refunds work for orders that have not shipped yet.

1. Cancel anytime before your order ships

You can cancel your pre-order at any time before it ships and we will refund you in full, including any shipping you paid, to your original payment method. No reason needed.

2. How to cancel

Email support@pilrsystems.com with your order number and tell us you would like to cancel. We will confirm by email once it is cancelled.

3. When you will be refunded

We start your refund as soon as we confirm the cancellation. Refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

4. Once your order has shipped

After your order ships it can no longer be cancelled, but our 30-day guarantee applies. See our returns and exchanges policy for how to return an item.

5. Contact us

Questions about a pre-order? Email support@pilrsystems.com or use our contact page.
