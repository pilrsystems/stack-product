Our Story

Is it really a routine if it isn't consistent?

Two friends, one shared problem — and a system built to solve it.

01 — The Starting Point

Built on routine.

Two friends who met in college and ended up living together. Our days were built around routine: training, studying, eating right, and showing up consistently.

Every morning started the same way, with coffee and our daily supplements. That was the anchor of our system.

We weren't obsessed with being perfect. We just knew that small, consistent habits added up. Each day was built on the one before it.

02 — The Problem

Travel kills consistency.

After graduating and starting work, that system was tested. Work meant lots of travel, and travel broke the routine. Without it, momentum slipped.

The problem was clear. Powders didn't fit in pill containers. Ziploc bags spilled. Cheap containers broke.

Nothing was built for the way we actually took supplements, powders, capsules, softgels, a full daily stack. So we built it.

03 — The Solution

How we built it.

We designed a system that could handle the way we actually live, structured, adaptable, and built to travel.

Not a container. A system. Built to adapt.

Eight months of design. Nine prototypes. Three pods, each with a purpose. Some people carry creatine and that's it. Some carry a whole lot more. 100% BPA-free Tritan, wrapped in an anodized aluminum shell built to last. Modular by design, so it fits your routine.

04 — The Standard

Why it matters.

Consistency is what drives results. Every day builds on the one before.

We built pilr so nothing gets in the way of that, no matter where you are.

— Andrea & Justin
